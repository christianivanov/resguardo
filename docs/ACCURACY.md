# Accuracy

How often the detectors find what they should, and how often they find something that is not there. Measured on a synthetic Spanish corpus; the numbers below are what `resguardo accuracy` prints today, and the CI fails if any of them gets worse ([ADR 0016](adr/0016-measuring-accuracy-and-latency.md)).

## Corpus

[`fixtures/accuracy/corpus.jsonl`](../fixtures/accuracy/corpus.jsonl): 406 texts, every value invented, generated by [`tools/accuracy/generate.py`](../tools/accuracy/generate.py) with a fixed seed. The generator computes check digits itself, independently of the Rust code.

- **306 negatives** that look sensitive and are not: order numbers, invoice references, postcodes, dates, licence plates, amounts, case numbers, commit hashes, UUIDs, ISBNs, tracking numbers, versions, IP addresses, plain prose, and DNIs, IBANs and cards with a wrong check digit. They include the examples of the design document's Annex G.6.
- **100 positives**, sensitive in format: DNI (also with dots), NIE, CIF, IBAN (printed and compact), cards, mobile and landline phones, email addresses, provider keys, and obfuscated values (Base64, full-width characters, zero-width characters). A few known gaps are included on purpose, so the misses are measured instead of hidden.

A commit hash, a UUID or a tracking number may be flagged as `secret_candidate` without counting as a false positive (Annex G.6); its default action only records it.

## Results

```console
$ resguardo accuracy
406 texts, 306 negatives

class              positives  found  missed false pos  FP rate
card                       9      9       0         2     0.7%
email                     10     10       0         0     0.0%
es_id                     39     36       3         1     0.3%
iban                      15     15       0         0     0.0%
invisible_text             6      6       0         0     0.0%
phone                     20     20       0         9     2.9%
secret                    15     15       0         0     0.0%

mistakes by group: gap_glued=3 isbn=11 uuid=1
```

The false-positive rate is over the 306 negatives. A false positive is a text where the class was found but was neither expected nor allowed.

## What the numbers say

- **Phones are the main source of false positives (2.9 %).** Nine digits starting with 6 to 9 are also references, and the ISBN prefix `84` followed by seven digits reads as a phone. The action for `phone` can be lowered to `flag` per principal.
- **Two ISBNs pass Luhn** by chance and are reported as cards: about one in ten 13-digit numbers does.
- **One UUID** contains a run that passes as a Spanish id.
- **The three misses are the known gap** of a value glued to letters (`DNI12345678Z`), which candidate windows never split (ADR 0010).
- Check digits do their job: none of the DNIs, IBANs or cards with a wrong check digit was reported.

## Reproducing and updating

```console
$ resguardo accuracy --baseline fixtures/accuracy/baseline.json     # what the CI runs
$ python3 -I tools/accuracy/generate.py                             # regenerate the corpus
$ resguardo accuracy --write-baseline fixtures/accuracy/baseline.json
```

Updating the baseline after a detector improves is a deliberate change to this file and to the baseline, in the same commit.

## Limits

The corpus is synthetic. Real traffic has other shapes, and the numbers here are a floor for regressions, not a promise about any particular workload.
